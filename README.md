### 💡 Executive Overview

I am a **Computer Science engineer and builder** operating at the convergence of **Machine Learning, Real-Time Computer Vision, Distributed Backend Architectures, and Object-Oriented Systems**.

My core passion lies in engineering systems where ML models move beyond isolated notebooks and survive the complexities of production:
- **Data Integrity & Evaluation**: Obsessed with root-cause validation—detecting target leakage, mitigating heavy class imbalances, optimizing precision-recall trade-offs, and avoiding false metric confidence.
- **Computer Vision & Edge Pipelines**: Building low-latency vision pipelines with YOLOv8, OpenCV, and streaming backends for real-world automated surveillance.
- **Backend Architecture**: Crafting asynchronous, role-based REST APIs and WebSocket layers using FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, and Supabase.
- **Foundational Software Engineering**: Solid grounding in Java, Data Structures & Algorithms, Clean Architecture, and Design Patterns.

### 🚀 Featured Systems & Flagship Repositories

#### 🛡️ [GuardianX](https://github.com/rushabh-borkar/GuardianX) — AI-Driven Emergency Detection & Incident Management Platform
> **Real-time multi-camera intelligent surveillance turning passive feeds into actionable emergency response.**
- **Architecture**: End-to-end event-driven architecture ingesting video streams, performing concurrent object detection with **YOLOv8** and **OpenCV**, and dispatching verified alerts via **FastAPI WebSockets**.
- **Capabilities**: Real-time incident categorization (fire, violence, hazard), incident log persistence with **SQLAlchemy**, and live geospatial visualization on a **React + TypeScript + Leaflet** operations dashboard.
- `Python` • `FastAPI` • `YOLOv8` • `OpenCV` • `React` • `TypeScript` • `WebSockets` • `Docker`

---

#### 🤝 [MentorMatch-AI](https://github.com/rushabh-borkar/MentorMatch-AI) — Explainable Student-Alumni Career Matching Engine
> **AI-powered mentorship ecosystem replacing black-box recommendations with transparent, deterministic scoring.**
- **Architecture**: Scalable role-based access control (`student`, `mentor`, `admin`) secured via **Supabase Auth**.
- **Capabilities**: Multi-factor matching algorithm analyzing skill sets, career trajectories, and gaps, rendering clear human-readable explanations behind every recommendation.
- `Python` • `FastAPI` • `Supabase` • `Recommendation Engine` • `RESTful API`

---

#### 🕸️ [GNN-Based Fraud Detection](https://github.com/rushabh-borkar/GNN-based-fraud-detection) & [Fraud Detection ML](https://github.com/rushabh-borkar/fraud-detection-ml)
> **Graph Neural Networks & Leakage-Proof Imbalance Classification for Financial Security.**
- **Focus**: Modeling relational graph topology across financial transactions and entities to uncover synthetic identity rings.
- **Methodology**: Solves severe class imbalance (>99:1 legitimate vs fraudulent ratio) utilizing PR-AUC, F-Beta weighting, and strict temporal split boundaries to eliminate look-ahead leakage.
- `Python` • `PyTorch Geometric` • `scikit-learn` • `Graph Neural Networks` • `Imbalanced Data`

---

#### 🏭 [Predictive Maintenance — Machine Failure Prediction](https://github.com/rushabh-borkar/Predictive-Maintenance-Machine-Failure-Prediction)
> **Condition-based industrial equipment monitoring to preempt costly operational downtime.**
- **Focus**: Analysis of continuous telemetry sensor feeds (rotational speed, torque, tool wear, heat dissipation).
- **Deliverable**: Supervised multivariate classification diagnosing pre-failure anomaly signatures and mapping failure modes.
- `Python` • `Jupyter` • `scikit-learn` • `Multivariate Modeling` • `Time-Series Telemetry`

---

#### ☄️ [Near-Earth Asteroid Hazard Classifier](https://github.com/rushabh-borkar/Near-Earth-Asteroid-Hazard-Classification-using-Logistic-Regression) — *Built From Scratch in NumPy*
> **Deep mathematical dive into optimization dynamics without high-level ML framework abstractions.**
- **Implementation**: Vectorized Logistic Regression engine with custom batch Gradient Descent, log-loss cost minimization, and precision-recall threshold tuning on NASA asteroid trajectory datasets.
- `Python` • `NumPy` • `Vectorized Math` • `Gradient Descent` • `Classification`
